# Blender models

Invoke the **blender** skill before changing a builder or a model: it holds the structure rules (supports reach their load,
+θ about x lowers the +z end, walls meet roofs, fixings within 2 cm, openings stay open), the build and compress steps, the
GLB contract and the Asset Row. Every build ends with `python3 tools/blender/structure_audit.py tools/blender/build_<name>.py`;
read every finding. A place model also follows the **places** skill.
